Q4 2025 signalled a reset in Canada’s multifamily market. Vacancy rose, new-lease rent growth cooled and performance became more uneven by metro. If you’re planning for 2026, this report offers the benchmarks that matter most: vacancy, rent trends, turnover, length of stay and digital prospect conversion.
